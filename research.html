Model training and evaluation

Efficient adaptation with biological priors

This project asks how domain knowledge can be introduced into pretrained protein models while preserving the efficiency benefits of parameter-efficient fine-tuning.

Bio-Informed LoRA for Signal Peptide Prediction

Research Assistant, eBRAIN Lab, NYU Abu Dhabi · 2026 to present

I implemented and evaluated an ESM-2 adaptation method that combines LoRA adapters with a separate projected path for BLOSUM62, hydrophobicity, and Grantham residue priors, added to hidden states before classification. In the original three-seed 650M comparison, BLOSUM62-guided LoRA had the highest mean across the reported primary metrics.

3 seedsused to confirm the reported result

Follow-up screensexploratory, not benchmark-comparable

My contribution focuses on training and evaluation design, including multi-seed protocols and a one-factor sensitivity analysis. Several promising single-seed gains did not replicate, reinforcing the need for controlled comparison.

Learning from code-mixed language data

This project compared task-specific multilingual fine-tuning with zero-shot general-purpose models on Hinglish named-entity recognition, where languages and scripts are mixed within the same data.

Hinglish Named Entity Recognition Benchmark

Research project · Fall 2024
